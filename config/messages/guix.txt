Guix is set up and its daemon is running, now and at every boot.

Log out and back in before you use it: /etc/profile.d/guix.sh is read at login,
and it is what puts the programs guix installs on your PATH. In this shell,
"source /etc/profile.d/guix.sh" does the same. Then, as root:

    guix install hello

Guix installs into /gnu/store and into per-user profiles, so nothing else on
this system is affected by anything you do with it. See
/root/sowa-howto/guix.txt.
