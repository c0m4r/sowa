Time
====

chronyd runs from boot and disciplines the clock against pool.ntp.org and
time.cloudflare.com. It can do nothing until there is a network, and it keeps
retrying until there is:

    chronyc tracking     # how far off the clock is, and from what
    chronyc sources -v   # the servers it is using
    chronyc makestep     # stop waiting and step the clock now

It is configured in /etc/chrony.conf and logs to /var/log/chronyd.log. Replace
the default time-source lines if the network has a time server of its own, then
apply it with:

    service chronyd restart

The time zone is /etc/localtime, a symlink into /usr/share/zoneinfo. Point it
somewhere else to change it:

    ln -sfn /usr/share/zoneinfo/Europe/Warsaw /etc/localtime

See also: network.txt, since chronyd can do nothing until there is a route.
